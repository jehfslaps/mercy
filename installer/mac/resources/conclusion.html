MERCY is installed.

Open your DAW. In Logic Pro and GarageBand, MERCY is under Audio Units › slapworx › Mercy. Other DAWs list it under slapworx after their plugin scan.

If Logic doesn't show it, open Logic › Settings › Plug-in Manager, select MERCY and click Reset & Rescan Selection.

The user manual (MERCY_User_Manual.pdf) is in the folder you downloaded.
