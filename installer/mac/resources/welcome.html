MERCY @VERSION@

A drum processor inspired by three eras of Kanye West: My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy, Watch the Throne and Yeezus.

This installs MERCY as an Audio Unit, a VST3 and a CLAP plugin for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer). On the next screens you can choose which ones you want.

Please quit your DAW before you continue.
